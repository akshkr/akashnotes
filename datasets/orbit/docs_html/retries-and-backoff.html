Retries and backoff

When a job attempt fails, Orbit schedules another attempt after a backoff delay. It keeps doing so until the job succeeds or runs out of attempts. A job that runs out is moved to the dead-letter queue with ORB-1301 RetryExhausted.

Global policy: [retries]​

The [retries] section of orbit.toml sets the default for every job:

[retries]
max_attempts = 3            # total attempts, including the first
backoff = "exponential"     # "exponential" | "linear" | "fixed"
max_delay_seconds = 600     # cap on any single backoff delay

These are also the built-in defaults, so a project with no [retries] section behaves exactly as shown.

Per-job policy: @app.job(retries=...)​

Override the policy for one job on its decorator:

@app.job(retries=5, backoff="exponential", timeout="30s")
def charge_card(payment_id: str): ...

@app.job(retries=2, backoff="fixed")
def refresh_avatar(user_id: str): ...

retries is the job’s maximum number of attempts and replaces max_attempts for that job. Both count the first attempt. retries=5 means at most five runs: the first plus four retries.

Precedence: decorator arguments override orbit.toml, which overrides the built-in defaults. backoff and retries can be overridden independently. max_delay_seconds is global only.

Backoff strategies​

Let n be the retry number (1 for the first retry). Before jitter, the delay is:

backoffDelay before retry nRetries 1–5
"exponential" (default)2n seconds2 s, 4 s, 8 s, 16 s, 32 s
"linear"10 × n seconds10 s, 20 s, 30 s, 40 s, 50 s
"fixed"10 seconds10 s, 10 s, 10 s, 10 s, 10 s

Every delay is capped at max_delay_seconds (600 s by default). With exponential backoff the cap kicks in at the tenth retry (210 = 1024 s → 600 s).

Jitter. Orbit then picks the actual delay uniformly between 50% and 100% of the computed value. A retry scheduled for “8 s” runs somewhere between 4 and 8 seconds later. Jitter stops a burst of jobs that failed together (say, during a database blip) from all retrying at the same instant. You can’t turn it off.

ℹ️note

The defaults are tuned for brief, transient failures: two retries within about six seconds. For flaky third-party APIs that go down for minutes at a time, raise the attempts (for example retries=10, which spreads nine retries over up to about 17 minutes) rather than the delay cap.

What counts as a failed attempt​

EventCounts as an attempt?Logged as
The job raises any exceptionYesthe exception
The attempt exceeds timeoutYesTimeoutError
The lease expired and the job was redeliveredYesORB-1101 LeaseExpired
The worker was killed for memory while running itYesORB-2101 WorkerOOM
Graceful shutdown handed the job back unstartedNon/a

The lease row deserves attention. A job that regularly outlives its lease never raises, yet each redelivery burns an attempt. With the default policy it lands in the DLQ after its third delivery, even though the code may have succeeded every time. If you see ORB-1301 preceded by ORB-1101, the fix is lease sizing, not retry tuning. See Leases and heartbeats.

Errors raised at enqueue time, such as ORB-1201 SerializationError, ORB-1202 PayloadTooLarge and ORB-2001 IdempotencyConflict, never reach the retry machinery. They are raised to the caller.

Permanent errors​

Orbit retries every exception. If an error can never succeed on retry (the record was deleted, the input is invalid), catch it in the job and return. Otherwise each such job burns its attempts and clutters the DLQ:

@app.job(retries=4)
def sync_invoice(invoice_id: str):
    try:
        invoice = billing.get_invoice(invoice_id)
    except billing.NotFound:
        log.info("invoice %s deleted before sync; skipping", invoice_id)
        return  # success: nothing left to do
    push_to_ledger(invoice)

What it looks like in the logs​

WARN  orbit.worker  job j_61c0aa charge_card attempt 1/5 failed:
      ConnectionResetError; retrying in 1.6s
WARN  orbit.worker  job j_61c0aa charge_card attempt 2/5 failed:
      ConnectionResetError; retrying in 3.1s
...
ERROR orbit.worker  [ORB-1301] RetryExhausted: job j_61c0aa charge_card
      failed 5/5 attempts; moved to DLQ (queue=default)

Attempt numbers count total attempts, so retries=5 shows /5.

Coming from 2.x​

In 2.x the global policy lived in the [retries] section of orbit.ini. Decorator arguments behave the same in both lines. orbit migrate-config orbit.ini converts the section to orbit.toml.

  • 3.x: orbit.toml
  • 2.x: orbit.ini
[retries]
max_attempts = 3
backoff = "exponential"
max_delay_seconds = 600
[retries]
max_attempts = 3
backoff = exponential
max_delay_seconds = 600
⚠️warning

orbit migrate-config in 3.0.1 silently dropped the [retries] section. Migrated projects fell back to the built-in defaults. This was fixed in 3.0.3. If you migrated with 3.0.1, open your orbit.toml and confirm [retries] is there. Re-running orbit migrate-config on 3.0.3 or later regenerates it.
