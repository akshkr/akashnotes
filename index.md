## Phase 1: How LLMs Work (Days 3–7)

**[Day 003: What an LLM Is (and Isn't)](Phase_1_How_LLMs_Work/Day_003_what_is_an_llm/what_is_an_llm.md)**  
A correct mental model prevents most beginner mistakes, such as expecting facts, determinism or memory. *You build:* a probe script that shows non-determinism, the knowledge cutoff, and a confident wrong answer.

**[Day 004: Tokens: How Models Read Text](Phase_1_How_LLMs_Work/Day_004_tokens/tokens.md)**  
Tokens drive cost, context limits and odd failures (spelling, numbers, other languages). *You build:* `count_tokens()` plus a back-of-envelope cost sheet. (The cost *tracker* comes in D12.)

**[Day 005: Inside the Transformer: Attention & Context Windows](Phase_1_How_LLMs_Work/Day_005_transformers_attention/transformers_attention.md)**  
Attention explains context limits, "lost in the middle", and why long prompts add latency. *You build:* a context-budget calculator, plus a **multi-needle-with-distractors** test (not the saturated single-needle test).

**[Day 006: Controlling Generation: Sampling & Reasoning Effort](Phase_1_How_LLMs_Work/Day_006_sampling_reasoning_effort/sampling_reasoning_effort.md)**  
You need to know which generation knobs exist on modern models, and which are now off-limits. *You build:* the simulators, plus an effort sweep that prints cost and latency.

**[Day 007: Choosing a Model: Capability, Cost, Latency & Lifecycle](Phase_1_How_LLMs_Work/Day_007_choosing_a_model/choosing_a_model.md)**  
Model choice is the biggest lever on cost and quality, and model IDs go stale within a single cohort. *You build:* the same task across tiers, graded on **10 hand-checked items (exact match)**, producing a cost/latency/accuracy table. It's redone with real evals in D20–21.

## Phase 3: Retrieval (RAG) (Days 23–34)

**[Day 023: Embeddings & Semantic Similarity](Phase_3_RAG/Day_023_embeddings/embeddings.md)**

**[Day 024: Ingesting Documents: Parsing PDFs, HTML & More](Phase_3_RAG/Day_024_document_ingestion/document_ingestion.md)**

**[Day 025: Chunking Strategies](Phase_3_RAG/Day_025_chunking/chunking.md)**

**[Day 026: Vector Search with pgvector](Phase_3_RAG/Day_026_pgvector_search/pgvector_search.md)**

**[Day 027: Hybrid Search: Keywords + Vectors](Phase_3_RAG/Day_027_hybrid_search/hybrid_search.md)**  
Vectors miss exact terms such as error codes and names. *You build:* `hybrid_search()` with RRF, compared against vector-only search on hit@k.

**[Day 028: Building the RAG Pipeline: Grounded Answers with Citations](Phase_3_RAG/Day_028_rag_pipeline/rag_pipeline.md)**  
The core pattern: retrieve, assemble, answer, cite. *You build:* `ask()`, which answers with citations.

**[Day 029: Evaluating Retrieval](Phase_3_RAG/Day_029_retrieval_evaluation/retrieval_evaluation.md)**  
Most bad RAG answers are retrieval failures. Measure before tuning. *You build:* a retrieval eval harness, plus a baseline report.

**[Day 030: Evaluating RAG Answers](Phase_3_RAG/Day_030_answer_evaluation/answer_evaluation.md)**  
A faithful answer and a relevant answer are different things. *You build:* an answer eval for `ask()`, added to the runner.

**[Day 031: Improving Retrieval: Reranking & Contextual Retrieval](Phase_3_RAG/Day_031_reranking_contextual_retrieval/reranking_contextual_retrieval.md)**  
These are the highest-leverage fixes, once you can measure. *You build:* a before/after table: vector → hybrid → +rerank → +contextual.

**[Day 032: Long Context vs RAG & Managed Retrieval](Phase_3_RAG/Day_032_long_context_vs_rag/long_context_vs_rag.md)**  
Sometimes the right answer is "don't build RAG at all". *You build:* the same questions answered three ways, comparing cost and quality.

**[Day 033: RAG over Structured Data: Text-to-SQL, Tables & Graphs](Phase_3_RAG/Day_033_text_to_sql/text_to_sql.md)**  
Much business data lives in databases, not documents. *You build:* a safe `ask_sql()` over the Atlas issues database (SQLite).

**[Day 034: Capstone R2: Ask, a RAG Assistant with a UI](Phase_3_RAG/Day_034_capstone_r2_ask/capstone_r2_ask.md)** 🏁  
Turn Phase 3 into a measured, cited assistant that other people can try. *You build:* **R2 Ask**: chat with citations, a before/after eval table, a demo of permission-filtered retrieval, and a public demo URL.
