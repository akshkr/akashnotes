# Curriculum Analysis

## Executive Summary

The 100-day AI engineering course is structurally sound and job-focused, with excellent capstone coherence and forward-progression within phases. However, five high-priority gaps exist that misalign the curriculum with 2026 production patterns: (1) **Agent memory architecture** lacks multi-layer design (short-term, working, long-term), (2) **Retrieval evaluation discipline** is scattered across three days with no unified metrics workflow, (3) **PII/data privacy in RAG** is conflated with API-key security rather than treated as a distinct operational concern, (4) **Phase 4 (multi-agent systems) is severely underfunded** (6 days) for a rapidly production-critical pattern, and (5) **Phase 5 has 3–4 days of known redundancy** that can be consolidated and rebalanced. The course has no critical ordering hazards, capstones are runnable and job-interview-ready, and redundancy is isolated to specific clusters (HITL×3, API-key security, persistence overlap). **All gaps can be addressed via in-place repurposing** without renumbering, freeing ~3 slots to address gaps while tightening Phase 5 and rebalancing phases.
